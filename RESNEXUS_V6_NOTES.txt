ResNexus v6 persistence fix

5361 resolves to Lil' Rustic with Hot Tub for 2026-10-22 through 2026-10-25.
The bug was that successful resolution of an ambiguous fan-out record was not
written into verifier diagnostics when the first fan-out copy already matched
the resolved resource/dates. If any other reservation failed verification,
the recovery layer then fell back to the raw fan-out snapshot.

This replacement makes resolved ambiguous records always emit a correction
diagnostic, allowing the existing recovery layer to collapse all fan-out copies
to the one verified resource.

No database migration is included.
